Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the Offline and Online editions?

Offline is a one-time payment that installs on a single device and never needs the internet day-to-day. Online is a subscription that syncs your data across every staff member and branch in real time, and still keeps working if you lose connection — it syncs automatically once you're back online.

Do I need to enter my card to start the free trial?

No. Every plan starts with a genuine 7-day free trial with full access — no card required. Your dashboard shows a countdown and an upgrade button the whole time.

What happens if my internet goes down?

Nothing stops. You can keep adding products, recording sales, and using every screen exactly as normal — everything is saved on your device immediately and syncs to the cloud automatically the moment you're back online.

Can I use it on my phone, or do I need a computer?

Either — OKV IMS installs like a normal app on Windows, Mac, Android, and iPhone/iPad, all from the same account. Many shop owners run the till entirely from a phone.

What does the 30-day money-back guarantee cover?

If you pay for a plan and it's not right for your business, tell us within 30 days of that payment and we'll refund it in full.

Can I try it before signing up for anything?

Yes — the Live Demo lets you click through the entire system with sample data, no signup needed, so you can see exactly how it works first.
