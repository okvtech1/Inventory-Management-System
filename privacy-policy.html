1. Who we are

OKV Inventory Management System is operated by OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect from organisations and users of the system ("you"), and how we use, store, and protect it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information you provide at signup: organisation name, admin name, email, phone, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text).
  • Business data you enter into the system: products, stock, sales, customers, suppliers, expenses, and related records — this belongs to your organisation.
  • Usage information such as login times and device/browser type, used for security and support.

3. How we use it

  • To provide and operate the system for your organisation.
  • To send account-related emails (welcome messages, password resets, billing/trial notices).
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot issues.
  • To improve the reliability and security of the system.

4. How your data is stored

Each organisation's business data is kept in a dedicated, isolated store; it is not shared with other organisations. Login credentials are kept centrally and separately from business data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your organisation's business data with third parties, except where required to operate the service (e.g. infrastructure providers) or where required by law.

6. Your choices

You can request a copy or deletion of your organisation's data, or close your account, by contacting us using the details below. Admins can also export their own data at any time using the system's built-in export/backup tools.
